# Reducing bad reviews before they happen

**What we found.** About 1 in 8 delivered orders gets a 1-2 star review. The main cause is late delivery: late orders get a bad review more than half the time, against 1 in 11 for on-time orders. It often starts with slow dispatch by the seller, visible as soon as the parcel reaches the courier.

**What we recommend.** Each week, score orders when they reach the courier and contact the riskiest 10%, about 650 orders a month, with a proactive message and a $5 voucher for their next purchase.

**What it could be worth.** If the contact prevents 20% of bad reviews in this group, that is about 38 fewer a month for around $3,250, or $87 per bad review avoided, about half the value of a typical targeted order. Note: The true effect could differ.

**How we will know.** Run a three-month test: half the selected orders get the contact, half do not. If bad reviews fall clearly in the contacted group without hurting repeat purchases, roll it out; if not, try the message without the voucher.
